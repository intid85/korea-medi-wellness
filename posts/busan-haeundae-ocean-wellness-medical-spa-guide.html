Overview

Haeundae pairs Centum City medical clinics with seawater spas and beachfront hotels — ideal for post-treatment recovery.

What to expect

Clinic visits in Centum, then thalasso, seawater pools and the Blueline coastal train.

Clinical note: Prices and outcomes vary. Always confirm the doctor's license, get a written quote, and disclose medications before any procedure.

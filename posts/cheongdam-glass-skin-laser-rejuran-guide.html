Overview

Cheongdam-dong is Seoul's premium dermatology hub. Glass-skin protocols combine pico laser toning, Rejuran Healer (PN injections) and skin boosters.

What to expect

A consultation, numbing cream (30–40 min), then 15–30 min treatment. Rejuran leaves small bumps for 1–2 days.

Clinical note: Prices and outcomes vary. Always confirm the doctor's license, get a written quote, and disclose medications before any procedure.

Recovery & aftercare

Avoid saunas and alcohol 48h, use cica cream and SPF50, and space laser sessions 2–4 weeks apart.
